Animation

Walt Disney Animation · Hexed

Disney is about to change its look

Speaking to Deadline at the Governors Awards, Disney Animation's chief creative officer Jared Bush said Hexed "may be" the studio's last original "in the Disney art style as people know it". Originals from Clay onwards will look "wildly different". Clay, due on 22 November 2028, is CG made to look like stop-motion. Sequels such as Zootopia 3 and Frozen 3 keep the familiar style.

Hexed opens in US cinemas on 25 November, with Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones and Jodie Foster in her first Disney animated film. Before it plays Lilo & Scratch, the studio's first theatrical short in five years. Early tracking pointed to $40–70 million over the five-day Thanksgiving weekend.

Via Creative Bloq · Pirates & Princesses
Anime · Netflix · 18 January

Sakamoto Days season 2 is dated

A main trailer and key visual confirm the return on 18 January 2027, on Netflix. TMS Entertainment animates again, now with Daisuke Nakajima as director, and Creepy Nuts sing the opening theme. Tomokazu Sugita still voices Taro Sakamoto.

Via Anime Corner · What's on Netflix
Disney · 5 February 2027

Ice Age heads for the volcano

The first trailer for Ice Age: Boiling Point brings back Ray Romano, John Leguizamo, Denis Leary, Queen Latifah and Simon Pegg's Buck. The herd must carry a dinosaur egg up an active volcano. John C. Donkin directs the sixth film.

Via Cartoon Brew

Series

Apple TV · Seasons 9 and 10

Slow Horses keeps galloping

On 16 November Apple renewed Gary Oldman's spy drama for two more seasons, less than a month after season six ended. Season seven, from Mick Herron's Bad Actors, is already filmed for 2027, and season eight, Clown Town, is in production for 2028.

That uses all nine published Slough House novels, so Apple has not said what seasons nine and ten will adapt. Days earlier Olivia Cooke had said she was shooting "the last season".

Via TVLine · Macworld

Film

US box office · 20–22 November

The Hunger Games catch fire again

Sunrise on the Reaping opened to an estimated $120 million in North America and $225 million worldwide. It is the franchise's best start since 2014 and its fourth-biggest ever, almost three times The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. Francis Lawrence directs, with Joseph Zada as the young Haymitch.

The rest of the chart

The prequel took about 72% of the weekend. I Play Rocky came second with $9.3 million and Ebenezer fell to third. Next up, Disney's Hexed and Focker-In-Law open on 25 November for Thanksgiving.

Via Yahoo Entertainment · Screen Rant
Universal · Back on 11 December

No director's cut for The Odyssey

Christopher Nolan says he stands by the film that went out in cinemas. The $1.8 billion epic, the biggest R-rated hit ever, returns to about 1,000 US screens.

Via AV Club
Lionsgate · Remake

Guadagnino drops American Psycho

Luca Guadagnino says the remake, written by Scott Z. Burns, is "not going ahead with me". He moves on to Camere separate with Léa Seydoux.

Via Espinof

Grammys · 7 February 2027

Raye leads, Rosalía follows

Nominations for the 2027 Grammys came out on 16 November. Raye leads with 11, Rosalía has nine, including Album of the Year for LUX, and Beyoncé reached her 100th career nomination.

Taylor Swift, Olivia Rodrigo, Bruno Mars and Phoebe Bridgers are also up for Album of the Year. The ceremony is on 7 February at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.

Video games

Rockstar · PS5 and Xbox Series

GTA VI breaks every record

Released on 19 November, Grand Theft Auto VI made $3.79 billion on day one, according to Take-Two, after 32.5 million pre-release sales. Reviews gave it a 99 on Metacritic, tying Ocarina of Time's all-time record.

Circana says over half of PS5 and Xbox players played it on launch day. A PC version has no date yet, and Take-Two is investigating a claimed leak of a development build.

Via Forbes · Notebookcheck · VGC

Books

SandersonCosmerePrizes
Brandon Sanderson · Tor Books

The Fires of December gets its hardcover

Sanderson's standalone Cosmere novel reaches US bookshops in hardcover on 8 December from Tor, after the ebook and the Michael Kramer audiobook came out on 6 October. December wakes on her 21st birthday with a lifetime of memories and travels the River Violet to warn the king's court about a coming plague.

It joins Tress of the Emerald Sea and Yumi and the Nightmare Painter in "Hoid's Travails", a set that can be read in any order. Backers of the Hoid's Storybook Collection campaign got it first, and the opening chapters can be read on the author's website.

Via Collider
Brandon Sanderson · Stormlight Archive

Stormlight 6 is being outlined

Sanderson is outlining books six to ten through 2027 and will start drafting book six in January 2028. It jumps ten years after Wind and Truth. There is no release date.

Via Collider
National Book Awards · New York

Paradiso 17 wins for fiction

Hannah Lillith Assadi won on 18 November. Spain's Vicente Luis Mora took translated literature for Centroeuropa, and Salman Rushdie received the lifetime medal.

Via Literary Hub
